Goal: the unit suite stops wasting wall time on Jest's file order and on compiling the same third-party crates of the `napi-addons` and `wasm-packages` fixtures for every test, without weakening any assertion (`VER-ARTIFACTS`, `VER-BUILD-INPUT-CHECK`). No `VER-` item changes.

Context: from the maintainer's audit of the test suites on 20 November 2026 (thread "Review and speed up nx-rust test suite"), measured at 9d3430a with `maxWorkers = 3` on a 32-core host and `NX_DAEMON=false`. The 11 heaviest unit specs took 323.0 s of wall time for 710.5 suite-seconds, an average concurrency of 2.20; 86.2 s were tail latency. `nx-rust/test/napi-build.spec.ts` alone took 162.9 s (15 tests) and was scheduled 10th of 11, which left two of three workers idle for about 125 s. It makes about 18 cold cargo builds of the 28 vendored crates under `napi-addons/registry/`, at 7 to 9 s each (slowest tests: lines 163-222 at 19.95 s, 575-599 at 18.89 s and 18.52 s, 642-710 at 16.24 s, 289-329 at 15.84 s).

- [ ] Longest-first order: Jest has no `testSequencer` here, so on a fresh checkout or in CI it sorts by file size and runs `napi-build.spec.ts` near the end. Add a small sequencer beside `nx-rust/jest.config.cts` that puts the compile-heavy specs first (`napi-build`, `batch-runs`, `build-input-check`, `cache-keys`, `target-defaults`, `caching-runs`, `workspace-build`, `wasm-build`, `affected-coverage`) and leaves the rest to Jest's default order (`super.sort`)
- [ ] One stable registry path: `copyFixture` (`nx-rust/test/harness/fixtures.ts`, around lines 196-199) copies the 2.9 MB `napi-addons/registry/` into every `$TMPDIR/nx-rust-napi-addons-XXXX/repo/registry`, and `rust/.cargo/config.toml` (line 7) names it as `local-registry = "../registry"`. Each copy therefore has another source identity for cargo, which compiles all 28 third-party crates again in every test. Point `local-registry` of each copy of `napi-addons` and `wasm-packages` at one stable read-only path (the fixture's own `registry/` folder) and stop copying `registry/` into the copies
- [ ] A pre-warmed `target/`: build a template `target/` lazily, once per test run, under `testRunFolder()` (for `napi-addons` the default host build and `--target <host>`, so that lines 289-329 reuse the dependencies as well; for `wasm-packages` the host build and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` where that target is installed), and clone it into each copy with reflinks where the file system has them (`cp -a --reflink=auto`, or `fs.copyFileSync` with `COPYFILE_FICLONE`; btrfs here, APFS on macOS, a plain copy on ext4 in CI). Remove the template in `globalTeardown` (`nx-rust/test/harness/global-teardown.ts`)
- [ ] Keep the crates of the workspace itself compiling fresh in every copy: either strip the members' artifacts and fingerprints from the template, or show that cargo fingerprints them again by the copy's absolute path, so that every `.d` file and every `NAPI_FORCE_BUILD_*` run is genuinely fresh. `napi-build.spec.ts` lines 458-486 ("leaves plain cargo to compile again only the napi crates and what depends on them") and every check of `VER-ARTIFACTS` and `VER-BUILD-INPUT-CHECK` must pass unchanged
- [ ] The `it.each(ordinary)` runs of `napi-addons` and `wasm-packages` in `nx-rust/test/build-input-check.spec.ts` (7.41 s and 6.33 s) get the benefit without a change of their own
- [ ] Combine the two `RUSTFLAGS` consumer cases (`napi-build.spec.ts` lines 575-599 and `nx-rust/test/wasm-build.spec.ts` lines 625-654): `it.each([[true, false], [false, true]])` creates two workspaces and runs `RUSTFLAGS="-C debuginfo=0"`, a full rebuild of 28 crates, twice (37.4 s). Put two JS consumer projects in one workspace, one with `{ dependentTasksOutputFiles: "**/*" }` and one without, so that one rebuild shows both the miss and the hit
- [ ] Show for each change that the tests still fail when the behaviour they guard is broken (a temporary mutation is enough), and give the timings before and after

Not to do: do not drop fixtures or cases, do not raise `maxWorkers` above 3, do not start long-lived Nx processes (`EVD-NX-24-PROCESS-STATE`), and keep every temporary file under `$TMPDIR`.

Done when: the specs named above pass with unchanged assertions, the measured wall time of `napi-build.spec.ts` and of the unit suite is reported (the audit expects about 25 to 35 s for `napi-build.spec.ts`), and `pnpm nx run-many -t build lint test`, `pnpm nx e2e nx-rust-e2e` and `pnpm nx format:check --all` pass.
